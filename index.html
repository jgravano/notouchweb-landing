What is NoTouchWeb?

NoTouchWeb is a browser-based gesture control interface for navigating digital content without touching a mouse, keyboard, or screen.

With camera permission, the current demo analyzes motion on the device and translates a hand wave into the next step of an on-screen guide. The camera feed is not uploaded, recorded, or stored.

What is NoTouchWeb?

NoTouchWeb is a browser-based gesture control interface for navigating digital content without touching a mouse, keyboard, or screen.

How does the demo work?

After you grant camera permission, the demo analyzes motion locally in the browser. A hand wave advances the on-screen coffee guide to its next step.

Does camera video leave my device?

No. In the current demo, camera frames are processed locally and are not uploaded, recorded, or stored.

Do I need special hardware?

No dedicated gesture hardware is required. The demo uses a camera already connected to your device and a modern browser with camera access.
